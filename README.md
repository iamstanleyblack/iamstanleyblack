## `03 / human.exe`

I love humans. I think people deserve dignity, safety, opportunity, and a fair chance to become who they want to be.

I have very little patience for bigotry, misogyny, or patriarchal standards that shrink people's lives. I value critical thinking, compassion, and a world where religious or any other dogma doesn't get to override someone's humanity.

I'm often the person who feels a lot but doesn't always show it. I value sincerity, mutual effort, and the energy people bring when they're present—not just when there's a screen between us.

I don't have the future all figured out, and honestly, I don't need to. I'd rather stay curious, do something meaningful today, and leave people and places a little better than I found them.

**My operating principles:** curiosity over certainty · empathy with boundaries · equal opportunity · build more than you judge.
